## Welcome to the Maestro Deck documentation.

Maestro Deck is a source-available runner for mobile end-to-end tests. Write your flows in YAML, run them locally or in CI, on iOS or Android, without a vendor lock-in.

## What's in here

- **E2E Testing**, the why behind end-to-end testing on mobile, and where Maestro fits.
- **Best Practices**, opinionated guidance to keep your suite fast and reliable.
- **Migration**, coming from Maestro Studio? Start here.
- **Reference**, CLI, YAML schema, and config file.

## Why Maestro Deck

- **Self-hosted**, your devices, your network, your data.
- **Source available**, no usage cap, no surprise pricing.
- **YAML-first**, flows are diffable, reviewable, and easy to share.
