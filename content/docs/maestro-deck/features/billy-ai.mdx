Billy AI is an assistant for your tests, you can choose to connect it through your own provider, we're following a BYOT approach.

We currently support Vertex AI and Anthropic as the only providers, you can add your credentials in the software's settings.

<Callout type="info">
  No credentials are ever sent to Maestro Deck, all your credentials are instead written to and read from your personal Keychain for your security.
</Callout>

## How it works

<div className="fd-steps">

### 1. Connect your provider

We currently support Vertex AI and Anthropic as the only providers, you can add your credentials in the software's settings.

### 2. Open Billy's panel

To open Billy's panel after configuring your credentials, click the star at the top of the toolbar.

### 3. Write tests in plain language

Billy AI lets you write your tests more simply, just using a sentence, or answer your questions about your tests' syntax.

### 4. Let Billy take the wheel

It's also able to take control via Maestro to run your tests, see your application's hierarchy, and re-run your tests or build them itself.

</div>
